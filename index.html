About Me

My path into tech started the way a lot of the best things do — out of curiosity, during a quiet pandemic afternoon, with a laptop and a question I couldn't stop asking: how does this actually work?
I'd spent years working as an Instrumentation Engineering Technologist — calibrating complex industrial systems, reading P&IDs, and troubleshooting problems that required both precision and patience. I loved the logic of it. But something was missing. When I stumbled into front-end development, I found it: a world where systems thinking meets creativity, where you can build something from nothing and actually see it work.
I completed the SheCodes Front-End Web Development Diploma and haven't stopped building since. I'm drawn to products that help people — tools for wellness, travel, learning, and growth. I believe great development isn't just about clean code; it's about understanding people well enough to build things they actually love using.
When I'm not coding, I'm probably listening to music, exploring somewhere new, or dreaming up the next thing I want to build. I'm quietly ambitious, always learning, and genuinely excited about what's next.
